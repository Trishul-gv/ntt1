Cancellation Policy

Plans change. Here is exactly how refunds work.

Get a Free Quote WhatsApp Us

Cancellation Timeline

  • More than 7 days before travel — full refund minus transaction charges
  • 4 to 7 days before travel — 75% refund
  • 2 to 3 days before travel — 50% refund
  • Within 24 hours or no-show — no refund

Rescheduling

You may reschedule once, free of charge, if you inform us at least 72 hours before departure and the new dates fall within six months. Rate differences during peak season may apply.

Third Party Bookings

Hotel, homestay, safari and jeep-ride cancellation charges are governed by the respective operator and are deducted from the refund at actuals.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.

Cancellation By Us

If we cancel a confirmed trip due to vehicle or operational reasons and cannot provide a replacement, you receive a full refund.
