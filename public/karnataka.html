Introduction

Discover Karnataka

Karnataka packs misty Western Ghats peaks, roaring waterfalls, Hoysala stone temples, tiger reserves and golden beaches into a single road trip. As a Chikkamagaluru based operator, this is our home ground — we drive these ghat roads every week and know exactly when each viewpoint is clear and each waterfall is full.

Popular Places

Places we cover in Karnataka

Chikkamagaluru

Coffee capital of Karnataka with Mullayanagiri, estate homestays and endless viewpoints.

Explore more

Coorg

Scotland of India — Abbey Falls, Dubare elephant camp and fragrant spice plantations.

Mysuru

Royal palace city with Chamundi Hills, Brindavan Gardens and a grand Dasara heritage.

Hampi

UNESCO World Heritage boulder landscape dotted with Vijayanagara temples and bazaars.

Jog Falls

India's dramatic four-stream waterfall at its thundering best right after the monsoon.

Belur

Chennakeshava Temple, the finest example of Hoysala sculpture and soapstone detail.

Halebidu

Hoysaleshwara and Kedareshwara temples with friezes carved over a century of work.

Bandipur

Tiger reserve on the Nilgiri fringe with excellent elephant and deer sightings.

Kabini

Backwater wildlife destination famous for leopards, black panthers and boat safaris.

Sringeri

Sharada Peetham on the Tunga river, a serene pilgrimage stop near Chikkamagaluru.

Kudremukh

Horse-face peak with rolling grasslands and one of Karnataka's finest treks.

Shivanasamudra

Twin waterfalls, Gaganachukki and Bharachukki, on the Kaveri river.

Gokarna

Temple town with quiet crescent beaches, Om Beach and clifftop sunsets.

Sakleshpur

Green ghat hills, coffee estates and the famous railway trek country.

Things To Do

Experiences worth planning around

  • Watch sunrise above the clouds at Mullayanagiri
  • Take a guided coffee estate and bean-to-cup tour
  • Jeep ride to Hebbe Falls and Jhari Falls
  • Jungle safari at Bhadra, Bandipur or Kabini
  • Trek Z Point, Kudremukh or Ballalarayana Durga
  • Explore Hoysala temples at Belur and Halebidu
  • Backwater rafting at Yagachi Dam
  • Sunset at Hirekolale Lake and Gokarna's Om Beach

Best Time To Visit

October to March is the most comfortable season across Karnataka, with clear skies and pleasant days. June to September is magical in the Malnad belt for waterfalls and mist, while November to February suits Hampi, Bandipur and Kabini best.

Travel Tips

  • Carry cash for jeep rides and small estate entry fees
  • Book Bhadra and Bandipur safaris well in advance
  • Fill fuel in town — ghat sections have very few pumps
  • Start peak drives by 5:30 AM for clear sunrise views
  • Respect plantation privacy and avoid plucking coffee cherries
