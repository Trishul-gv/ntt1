Popular Places

Places we cover in Karnataka


Chikkamagaluru

Chikkamagaluru is a beautiful hill town in Karnataka, famous for its lush coffee plantations, misty mountains, and pleasant climate. Nestled in the Western Ghats, it attracts nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts. Popular attractions include Mullayanagiri Peak, Baba Budangiri, and Hebbe Falls. The region is also renowned for its coffee production and scenic landscapes.

Explore more

Madikeri

Madikeri is a beautiful hill station in Karnataka and the headquarters of Kodagu district. Surrounded by green hills, coffee plantations, and misty landscapes, it offers a peaceful escape for nature lovers. Popular attractions include Raja's Seat, Abbey Falls, and Madikeri Fort. The region is also known for biodiversity and scenic beauty.


Coorg

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is a beautiful hill destination in Karnataka, famous for its coffee plantations, misty mountains, and lush green forests. Popular attractions include Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat, and Dubare Elephant Camp. Known for its pleasant climate, rich culture, and scenic landscapes, Coorg attracts nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.


Sakleshpur

Sakleshpur is a picturesque hill station in Karnataka, renowned for its coffee plantations, green hills, and scenic landscapes. Surrounded by the Western Ghats, it offers beautiful views and a peaceful atmosphere. Popular attractions include Manjarabad Fort, Bisle View Point, and Jenukallu Gudda. Sakleshpur is ideal for trekking, sightseeing, and nature lovers.


Mysuru

Mysore, officially known as Mysuru, is a historic city in Karnataka famous for magnificent palaces, rich cultural heritage, and royal traditions. Once the capital of the Kingdom of Mysore, it is home to the iconic Mysore Palace. The city is also celebrated for its cleanliness, traditional festivals, silk sarees, and delicious Mysore Pak.


Mangaluru

Mangaluru, formerly known as Mangalore, is a major coastal city in Karnataka, situated between the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats. It is famous for its busy port, beautiful beaches, historic temples, and diverse cultural traditions. The city is an important commercial centre and transportation hub, offering delicious coastal cuisine, scenic seaside attractions, and a rich cultural heritage that attracts tourists from across India.


Dharmasthala

Dharmasthala is a renowned pilgrimage town in Karnataka, famous for the historic Manjunatha Temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. Situated near the Netravati River, it attracts thousands of devotees throughout the year. The town is known for religious harmony, charitable activities, and traditional hospitality. Nearby attractions include the Bahubali statue and peaceful natural surroundings.


Kukke Subramanya

Kukke Subramanya is a sacred pilgrimage destination in Karnataka's Western Ghats. The town is famous for Kukke Subramanya Temple, dedicated to Lord Subramanya, worshipped as a protector against serpent-related afflictions. Surrounded by lush forests and beautiful mountains, it attracts devotees and nature lovers. The destination offers spiritual experiences and peaceful natural surroundings.


Kollur Mookambika

Kollur Mookambika is a famous pilgrimage destination in Karnataka, nestled at the foothills of the scenic Kodachadri Hills. The renowned Mookambika Temple is dedicated to Goddess Mookambika, representing divine feminine energy. Surrounded by dense forests and the Souparnika River, the town attracts devotees year-round and is celebrated for its spiritual significance and natural beauty.


Udupi

Udupi is a coastal city in Karnataka, renowned for its Krishna Temple, spiritual traditions, and vegetarian cuisine. Located near the Arabian Sea, it offers attractions such as Malpe Beach and St. Mary's Islands. Udupi is also an important educational centre, attracting pilgrims, students, and tourists seeking cultural experiences, scenic landscapes, and South Indian food.


Murdeshwar

Murdeshwar is a coastal town in Karnataka, famous for its Shiva statue and historic Murudeshwara Temple. Located along the Arabian Sea, it offers ocean views, sandy beaches, and beautiful sunsets. The towering statue of Lord Shiva attracts pilgrims and tourists. Visitors can also enjoy water activities and explore nearby coastal landscapes.


Gokarna

Gokarna is a charming coastal town in Karnataka, known for sacred temples, peaceful beaches, and natural beauty. The famous Mahabaleshwar Temple attracts devotees seeking blessings from Lord Shiva. Popular beaches include Om Beach, Kudle Beach, and Half Moon Beach. Combining spiritual significance with scenic coastal landscapes, Gokarna welcomes pilgrims, beach lovers, and travellers seeking tranquillity.


Badami

Badami is a historic town in Karnataka, renowned for its rock-cut cave temples and ancient Chalukya architecture. Once the capital of the Badami Chalukya dynasty, it features striking sandstone cliffs, beautiful Agastya Lake, and historic shrines. Visitors can explore intricate sculptures, ancient temples, and archaeological wonders while discovering the region's cultural heritage and architectural achievements.


Pattadakal

Pattadakal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Karnataka, celebrated for its collection of ancient Hindu and Jain temples. Situated along the Malaprabha River, it showcases architectural styles developed during the Chalukya dynasty. Intricate carvings, sculpted pillars, and stone structures attract history enthusiasts. Pattadakal offers visitors a glimpse into India's medieval architecture and religious traditions.


Aihole

Aihole is an ancient village in Karnataka, famous for its collection of Hindu, Jain, and Buddhist monuments. Considered an important birthplace of Indian temple architecture, it contains temples dating back to the Chalukya period. Visitors can explore intricate carvings, historic shrines, and archaeological remains while discovering the artistic achievements and cultural heritage of ancient Karnataka.


Kudalasangama

Kudalasangama is an important pilgrimage destination in Karnataka, renowned as the meeting point of the Krishna and Ghataprabha rivers. It is closely associated with Basavanna, philosopher and founder of the Lingayat movement. Visitors can explore the Sangameshwara Temple and Basavanna's Aikya Mantapa. Its peaceful riverside setting and religious significance make it a meaningful spiritual destination.


Bijapur

Bijapur, officially known as Vijayapura, is a historic city in northern Karnataka, famous for monuments from the Adil Shahi dynasty. Its celebrated attraction is Gol Gumbaz, renowned for its enormous dome and whispering gallery. The city also features Ibrahim Rauza, historic mosques, and impressive stone architecture, offering insights into medieval Indian history and culture.


Belgaum

Belgaum, officially known as Belagavi, is a historic city in northern Karnataka, surrounded by the scenic Western Ghats. It is known for ancient forts, temples, and rich cultural heritage. Belgaum Fort and the magnificent Kamal Basadi are notable attractions. The city is an important educational and industrial centre, connecting Karnataka with Maharashtra and Goa.


Hubli

Hubli, also known as Hubballi, is a major commercial city in Karnataka and forms a twin-city region with Dharwad. It serves as an important transportation, business, and educational hub in northern Karnataka. The city is known for bustling markets, historic temples, and growing infrastructure. Visitors can explore nearby cultural attractions and enjoy local cuisine.


Hampi

Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Karnataka, famous for the ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire. Its landscape features ancient temples, enormous boulders, royal enclosures, and intricately carved monuments. Popular attractions include Virupaksha Temple, Vittala Temple, and the iconic Stone Chariot. Hampi offers travellers a memorable journey through India's medieval architectural and cultural heritage.


Chitradurga

Chitradurga is a historic city in Karnataka, renowned for its hilltop fort and stories of courage. Chitradurga Fort features massive stone walls, ancient gateways, and defensive structures spread across rocky hills. Visitors can explore historic temples, scenic landscapes, and architectural remains while discovering the region's heritage. The city appeals to history lovers and travellers.

Things To Do

Experiences worth planning around

  • Watch sunrise above the clouds at Mullayanagiri
  • Take a guided coffee estate and bean-to-cup tour
  • Jeep ride to Hebbe Falls and Jhari Falls
  • Jungle safari at Bhadra, Bandipur or Kabini
  • Trek Z Point, Kudremukh or Ballalarayana Durga
  • Explore Hoysala temples at Belur and Halebidu
  • Backwater rafting at Yagachi Dam
  • Sunset at Hirekolale Lake and Gokarna's Om Beach

Best Time To Visit

October to March is the most comfortable season across Karnataka, with clear skies and pleasant days. June to September is magical in the Malnad belt for waterfalls and mist, while November to February suits Hampi, Bandipur and Kabini best.

Travel Tips

  • Carry cash for jeep rides and small estate entry fees
  • Book Bhadra and Bandipur safaris well in advance
  • Fill fuel in town — ghat sections have very few pumps
  • Start peak drives by 5:30 AM for clear sunrise views
  • Respect plantation privacy and avoid plucking coffee cherries
