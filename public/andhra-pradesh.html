Introduction

Discover Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh blends the sacred and the scenic — Tirumala's temple hills, the coffee slopes of Araku, India's own Grand Canyon at Gandikota and a long, breezy Bay of Bengal coastline. Our drivers handle the long highway stretches so you arrive rested.

Popular Places

Places we cover in Andhra Pradesh

Tirupati

Sri Venkateswara temple at Tirumala, one of the world's most visited pilgrimage sites.

Araku Valley

Coffee-scented hill valley reached by one of India's most scenic train and road routes.

Lambasingi

The Kashmir of Andhra, where winter temperatures occasionally touch zero.

Gandikota

Spectacular Pennar river gorge beside a historic fort, perfect for camping.

Lepakshi

Vijayanagara temple town famous for the hanging pillar and giant monolithic Nandi.

Srisailam

Jyotirlinga temple set inside the Nallamala forest and tiger reserve.

Borra Caves

Million-year-old limestone caves with dramatic stalactite formations.

Visakhapatnam

Vizag's beaches, submarine museum and the hilltop Kailasagiri park.

Vijayawada

Kanaka Durga temple, Prakasam Barrage and the Undavalli rock-cut caves.

Horsley Hills

Quiet eucalyptus-covered hill retreat near the Karnataka border.

Things To Do

Experiences worth planning around

  • Book a Tirumala darshan slot and enjoy the temple hills
  • Ride the Kirandul train route to Araku Valley
  • Camp on the rim of Gandikota gorge
  • Explore the Borra limestone caves
  • Walk Vizag's Rushikonda and Rama Krishna beaches
  • Catch a frosty sunrise at Lambasingi

Best Time To Visit

October to March offers pleasant weather statewide. Lambasingi is coldest and most beautiful from December to January, while Araku Valley stays green and cool from June through February.

Travel Tips

  • Pre-book Tirumala darshan and accommodation online
  • Carry warm clothing for Lambasingi even in summer
  • Start early for the Araku ghat road to avoid mist delays
  • Coastal sun is strong — carry sunscreen and water
