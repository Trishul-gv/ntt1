Introduction

Discover Maharashtra

Maharashtra spans Sahyadri hill stations, rock-cut world heritage caves, the Shirdi pilgrimage and two of India's most energetic cities. Great for monsoon travel when the whole ghat belt turns emerald.

Popular Places

Places we cover in Maharashtra

Mahabaleshwar

Strawberry farms, Arthur's Seat and panoramic Sahyadri valley points.

Lonavala

Tiger's Leap, Bhushi Dam and monsoon waterfalls between Mumbai and Pune.

Panchgani

Table Land plateau, Sydney Point and colonial-era boarding-school charm.

Mumbai

Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Elephanta Caves and legendary street food.

Pune

Aga Khan Palace, Shaniwar Wada and a lively café and fort-trek culture.

Shirdi

Sai Baba Samadhi Mandir, one of India's busiest pilgrimage centres.

Ajanta

Second-century BCE Buddhist caves with extraordinary surviving murals.

Ellora

Kailasa temple carved from a single rock — a UNESCO masterpiece.

Alibaug

Beach town escape from Mumbai with Kolaba Fort and quiet shores.

Matheran

Asia's only vehicle-free hill station, explored on foot, horseback or toy train.

Things To Do

Experiences worth planning around

  • Sunrise at Mahabaleshwar's Wilson Point
  • Monsoon waterfall drives around Lonavala
  • Guided tour of Ajanta and Ellora caves
  • Darshan at Shirdi Sai Baba temple
  • Marine Drive evening walk in Mumbai
  • Toy train ride up to Matheran

Best Time To Visit

October to February is the most comfortable time. June to September is spectacular for waterfalls and green valleys in Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar and Matheran.

Travel Tips

  • Ajanta caves are closed on Mondays, Ellora on Tuesdays
  • Book Shirdi darshan passes online to save hours
  • Ghat roads get slippery in heavy monsoon — allow extra time
  • Matheran does not allow vehicles beyond Dasturi Point
