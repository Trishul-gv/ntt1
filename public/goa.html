Introduction

Discover Goa

Goa is beaches, Portuguese heritage, river cruises and the greenest corner of the Western Ghats all within an hour of each other. We run comfortable road trips from Chikkamagaluru and Bengaluru with flexible beach-hopping days.

Popular Places

Places we cover in Goa

North Goa

Buzzing beach belt with markets, shacks, water sports and nightlife.

South Goa

Peaceful palm-lined beaches, luxury resorts and slow sunsets.

Baga Beach

Water sports, beach shacks and Goa's liveliest evening scene.

Calangute

The Queen of Beaches — wide sands and every kind of activity.

Palolem

Crescent bay in South Goa known for kayaking and silent discos.

Anjuna

Famous Wednesday flea market and dramatic rocky shoreline.

Vagator

Cliff-top views, Ozran beach and legendary sunset parties.

Fort Aguada

17th-century Portuguese fort and lighthouse over the Mandovi mouth.

Chapora Fort

Hilltop ruin with sweeping views over Vagator beach.

Dudhsagar Falls

Four-tiered milk-white waterfall reached by jeep through Bhagwan Mahaveer sanctuary.

Things To Do

Experiences worth planning around

  • Jeep safari to Dudhsagar Falls
  • Sunset cruise on the Mandovi river
  • Old Goa church circuit including Basilica of Bom Jesus
  • Parasailing and jet-ski at Baga or Calangute
  • Spice plantation lunch near Ponda
  • Kayaking at Palolem and dolphin spotting

Best Time To Visit

November to February brings perfect beach weather and festive nightlife. October and March are quieter with better rates, while the monsoon months are lush and dramatic for Dudhsagar.

Travel Tips

  • Carry a valid licence if you plan to rent a scooter
  • Beach shack rates rise sharply in late December
  • Dudhsagar entry is by authorised jeep only
  • Respect no-swim red flags during rough seas
